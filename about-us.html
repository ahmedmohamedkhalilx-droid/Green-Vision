ABOUT GREEN VISION CONTRACTING LLC

Green Vision Contracting LLC (GVC) was established in Abu Dhabi in 2010. Our company has grown dramatically in response to our projects and market requirements. From a small company with a handful of employees, our workforce now surpasses 100 persons ranging from laborers to senior engineers and management staff with proven track records in the electromechanical industry.


Our local set up includes a head office which houses management, financial, administration, human resources and tendering departments.


A separate site office is set up at each project location housing the project manager and engineers, QS, and draughting team. In addition, we maintain and operate our own workers camp to ensure quality lodging and facilities for our employees.


Within the field of Engineering and Construction, Green Vision carries out various MEP installation work, as well as maintenance, testing and commissioning activities. Green Vision offers its capabilities as a single service or multi-services engineering contractor for both large and small contracts. Through our experienced onsite personnel, the expertise necessary in Project Management, Planning and Coordination, Supervision of Sub-Contractors, Purchasing and Procurement, Labor Management, Quality Control, Commissioning, Project Handover and Maintenance are presented and delivered as scheduled.

MISSION & VISION

To be an exceptionally competent firm in Project Management, Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing, Green Vision offers our clients the highest quality standards in our areas of expertise by applying the best international practices and project specifications, providing world-class innovative engineering and technical solutions with excellence of products.


Working together with our partners, we will be able to provide a turnkey package, from the initial consultation and service to the final supply and installation of the electrical equipment and spare parts.


To establish the organization's leadership in both Electrical Engineering and preferred-services companies in the energy, Transmission & Distribution market, and the Oil & Gas market, with a consciousness of responsibility for society and the environment throughout the GCC region and the Middle East.


To aggressively leverage our unique set of well-trained staff to provide exceptional value for our clients.
