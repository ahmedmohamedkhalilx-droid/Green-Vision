About Green Vision Contracting

Green Vision Contracting LLC (GVC) was established in Abu Dhabi in 2010. The company has grown in response to its projects and market requirements, from a handful of employees to a workforce of more than 100, ranging from laborers to senior engineers and management staff with proven track records in the electromechanical industry.

The head office houses management, finance, administration, human resources and tendering. Each project location has its own site office for the project manager, engineers, QS and draughting team. The company also maintains and operates its own workers camp, so employees have quality lodging and facilities.

In engineering and construction, Green Vision carries out MEP installation, maintenance, testing and commissioning, as a single-service or multi-service contractor on large and small contracts. Experienced onsite personnel deliver project management, planning and coordination, supervision of subcontractors, purchasing and procurement, labor management, quality control, commissioning, handover and maintenance as scheduled.

Mission and vision

To be an exceptionally competent firm in project management, mechanical, electrical and plumbing works, offering clients the highest quality standards through international best practice, project specifications and engineering solutions backed by quality products.

Working with partners, to provide a turnkey package from initial consultation to the final supply and installation of electrical equipment and spare parts.

To establish leadership in electrical engineering and preferred services for the energy, transmission and distribution, and oil and gas markets across the GCC and the Middle East, with responsibility for society and the environment.

To use a well-trained team to provide exceptional value for clients.
