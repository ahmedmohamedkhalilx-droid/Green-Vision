Welcome toGREEN VISION CONTRACTING LLC.

Green Vision Contracting LLC (GVC) was established in Abu Dhabi in 2010. Our company has grown dramatically in response to our projects and market requirements. From a small company with a handful of employees, our workforce now surpasses 100 persons ranging from laborers to senior engineers and management staff with proven track records in the electromechanical industry.

Our local set up includes a head office which houses management, financial, administration, human resources and tendering departments. A separate site office is set up at each project location housing the project manager and engineers, QS, and draughting team. In addition, we maintain and operate our own workers camp to ensure quality lodging and facilities for our employees.
