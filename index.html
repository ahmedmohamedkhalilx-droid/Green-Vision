About Green Vision

Green Vision Contracting LLC was established in Abu Dhabi in 2010. From a small company with a handful of employees, our workforce now surpasses 100 people, from laborers to senior engineers and management staff with proven track records in the electromechanical industry.

Each project location has its own site office with a project manager, engineers, QS and a draughting team, and we run our own workers camp.

Mission and vision

To be an exceptionally competent firm in project management, mechanical, electrical and plumbing works, offering clients the highest quality standards through international best practice, project specifications and engineering solutions backed by quality products.

To use a well-trained team to provide exceptional value for clients.
