Careers

Work with Green Vision

Green Vision Contracting grew from a handful of employees to a team of more than 100, from laborers to senior engineers and management staff. Each project has its own site office with a project manager, engineers, QS and draughting team, and the company runs its own workers camp.

We are interested in hearing from engineers, quantity surveyors, draughtsmen, supervisors, technicians and skilled labour in mechanical, electrical and plumbing works.

Open positions

No vacancies are posted right now. Send us your CV and we will keep it on file for upcoming projects.

How to apply

  1. Fill in the form with your name, contact details and the role you are looking for.
  2. Your email app opens with your details ready. Attach your CV (PDF or Word) and send it.
  3. Our team reads every CV and contacts you when a suitable role opens.

You can also email your CV directly to info@greenvision.ae.
