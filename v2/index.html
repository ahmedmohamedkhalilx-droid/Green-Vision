About us

From a handful of people to 100+ professionals.

Green Vision Contracting LLC (GVC) was established in Abu Dhabi in 2010 and has grown in step with its projects and the market. Our workforce now surpasses 100 people, from laborers to senior engineers and management with proven track records in the electromechanical industry.

Management, finance, HR and tendering sit in our head office; each project gets its own site office with a project manager, engineers, QS and draughting team. We also run our own workers camp, so crews are housed and supported properly.
